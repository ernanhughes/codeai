## Rescues and hardness

- H1-only rescues (3): `v2-strip-query` (mistral only — genuine diversity rescue),
  `v2-half-up-rounding` (qwen draw inside H1 — sampling luck, not diversity),
  `v2-keyed-cache-length` (all three H1 draws; 0/3 C1 draws — ambiguous).
- C1-only rescue (1): `v2-splitlines-cr`.
- C0-only solves (2): `v2-lsp-square`, `v2-unsound-cache` — a single draw beating
  six combined C1+H1 draws. Sampling noise cuts both ways.
- Unsolved by every arm (4): `v2-registry-pollution`, `v2-retry-once-accepted`,
  `v2-size-format`, `v2-stable-priority` — the new hard core.

## Preregistered interpretation applied

Rule 1 threshold ("materially beats") not met: +0.05 at n=40 with per-stratum
n=10 is a weak positive, not a material win. Rule 2 therefore still governs the
default — **homogeneous sampling remains the default** — but with a new
qualification P1 could not provide: heterogeneity shows its only gains where
tasks are easy enough to be solvable yet hard enough to vary (local/boundary),
and per-candidate H1 is strictly weaker. Rule 5 fires for the 4-task hard core.

## Limits

Per-stratum n=10: stratum table is suggestive, not conclusive. Local models only;
cloud-model diversity untested. Cost unknown (tokens/latency only).
